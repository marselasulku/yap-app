# Yap Support

Yap About Your Day is a daily journal that fills in the day for you: where you were, the weather, sunrise and sunset, and holidays. You add the writing, voice notes, photos and short lists.

## Common questions

**Where is my journal stored?**
On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud account if you're signed in to iCloud. There are no Yap accounts, and nobody else can read it.

**My entries aren't showing up on my other device.**
Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Yap (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes, especially for photos and videos.

**The place or weather is missing for today.**
Yap only uses your location if you allow it. On today's page, tap "Turn on location", or allow it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → yap. Weather also needs an internet connection.

**My voice note wasn't turned into text.**
Allow Speech Recognition for Yap in the Settings app. If your iPhone can't transcribe on-device, transcription needs an internet connection.

**How do I delete everything?**
In Yap, tap the gear on Today, then "Delete all entries". This removes your journal from this device and from iCloud.
