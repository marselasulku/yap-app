# Yap Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Yap is a private journal. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer can't see what you write.

## What stays with you

Everything you add to Yap stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account: your writing, voice notes and their transcripts, photos, videos, lists, the name you give it and your answers during setup. If you're signed in to iCloud, Yap syncs your journal through your private iCloud database so it's backed up and available on your other devices. That data is stored by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and only you can read it. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your journal stays only on your device.

## What leaves your device, and why

Yap uses a few outside services to fill in details about each day. None of them receive anything you write.

| Service | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) | An approximate position (to about 1 km) and the date | To look up that day's weather, sunrise and sunset |
| [Nager.Date](https://date.nager.at) | A country code and the year | To look up that country's public holidays |
| Apple (location services) | Your position, handled by iOS | To turn your position into a city and country name |
| Apple (speech recognition) | A voice note's audio, only when your iPhone can't transcribe it on-device | To turn the voice note into text |

These requests aren't tied to your identity, and Yap doesn't send any identifier with them. The services may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy policies.

## Permissions

Yap only asks for what a feature needs, and each one is optional:

- **Location (while using the app):** to record the city you're in and look up the weather and holidays. Yap doesn't track your location in the background.
- **Photos:** to suggest the photos and videos you took on a day. Only the items you add are copied into your journal.
- **Microphone and speech recognition:** to record voice notes and transcribe them.

You can change these at any time in the Settings app.

## Deleting your data

In Yap, go to **Settings → Delete all entries** to remove everything from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. You can also manage Yap's iCloud data in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**.

## Children

Yap doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
